03AI, deliberately

AI that stays
inside the lines.

AI can take real work off your team — answering repetitive questions, making sense of messy data, keeping routine jobs moving. In a website or an app, we decide exactly where it is used and what it is allowed to touch — and we tell you both before we start.

Where it earns its place9 uses
  • 01
    Customer queriesStraight answers drawn from your own pages, policies, and prices, with a clear hand-off to a person when the question needs one.
  • 02
    Admin assistantsSummaries, record lookups, and drafted replies inside the web or mobile app your team already works in.
  • 03
    Data analysisReports, trends, and anomalies from your own numbers. The model and the depth are chosen to fit the question and the budget.
  • 04
    Background jobsScheduled digests, categorisation, and follow-ups that would otherwise be done by hand, late, or not at all.
  • 05
    Document & form extractionStructured data pulled out of invoices, applications, and uploads, then checked before anything is stored.
  • 06
    Semantic searchFinding the right page, product, or record from a question written the way people actually talk.
  • 07
    Lead triageEnquiries qualified, tagged, and routed to the right desk before a person picks them up.
  • 08
    Translation & localisationContent adapted for other languages and markets without a human retyping every page.
  • 09
    Anomaly alertsUnusual orders, traffic, or data patterns flagged for review — a prompt to look, not a verdict.
How we keep it contained6 rules
  • ✓
    Tool allowlistThe model only sees the tools we hand it. Everything else is out of reach, whatever it is asked to do.
  • ✓
    Read-only by defaultWriting, sending, and charging need a permission we agree on with you first.
  • ✓
    Human approvalConsequential actions wait for a person to confirm them. Nothing important happens on a model say-so.
  • ✓
    Your data stays yoursNo training on your content, and no sending private prompts or records anywhere they do not need to go.
  • ✓
    Self-hosted when privacy mattersOllama or LM Studio on your own infrastructure. Your data never leaves your systems, and we handle the setup.
  • ✓
    Visible and limitedLogs, usage caps, and cost limits, so what the AI did and what it cost is never a mystery.

✳ Already running an AI provider in another app? Tell us which one and we can reuse it — you give us the provider, we handle the integration. If privacy is the concern, we will run it on your own infrastructure instead.

05Scoping

The right size,
not the biggest.

A good build should be an investment, not an obligation. We scope around the outcome you need now, then plan the rest for when it makes sense.

Start with the outcome

We begin with what the product needs to do for the business, not with a long list of features. Fewer, better decisions.

If a feature does not help a customer or save you time, it does not belong in the first version. We would rather do six things well.
